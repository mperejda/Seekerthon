How it works

Three roles, one trustless cycle. Every step is enforced by the smart contract.

1

Organizer funds escrow

Deposit USDC into a Solana escrow program. Funds are locked on-chain immediately — the organizer cannot touch them until voting ends.

For organizers
2

Builders submit projects

Alpine Labs Builder Pass holders submit their projects — demo video, repo, and description — during the open submission window.

For builders
3

Community votes & wins

Seeker Genesis holders vote with $SKR-weighted power via the Android app. When voting closes, the organizer releases the prize on-chain to the winners.

For voters

Why Seekerthon

Not another hackathon platform. Built specifically for the Seeker ecosystem with on-chain accountability at every step.

Trustless escrow

Prize money is locked in a Solana program the moment a hackathon is funded. Nobody can rug the prize pool.

$SKR-weighted votes

Vote power scales with your $SKR balance and staked amount. Deeper holders have more say in who wins.

Builder Pass gated

Submissions are gated to Alpine Labs Builder Pass holders, keeping the quality bar high.

Native Android voting

A TikTok-style voting feed built for Seeker community members to discover and vote on projects on the go.
